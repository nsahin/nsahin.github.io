Nil Sahin

I work as a Senior Data Scientist at Recursion Pharmaceuticals, by helping with our scientific software platforms to enable drug discovery. I enjoy implementing new scientific algorithms and workflows, building computational tools and working in teams. I am proud of applying my science knowledge to healthcare, helping with combating diseases and improving the quality of our lives.
